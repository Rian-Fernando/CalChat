CalChat · Brand guide · v1.0

Two schedules,
one overlap.

CalChat is a calendar tool for friends in different time zones. The mark — two outlined squares meeting at a terracotta intersection — is the brand idea made visual. The wordmark stays quiet so the mark can carry the meaning.

01Primary lockup

Use this in almost every context. The mark sits to the left, the wordmark sits to its right. Don't separate them by more than the mark's width.

02Variations

Pick the variant that matches your surface. Color → paper, light → dark, mono → restrained contexts where a single ink reads cleaner.

04Wordmark

Lowercase Inter Tight 700, letter-spacing −0.04em. The wordmark sits on its own only in long-form contexts (legal pages, body copy) where the mark would be redundant.

07Clear space

Keep a perimeter equal to 20% of the mark's height free of other elements. Use the inner accent square as your unit if you need to eyeball it.

08Don't

A handful of failure modes to actively avoid. The brand idea is fragile in only a few specific ways.

Don'tRotate or skew the mark.
Don'tStretch the lockup.
Don'tDrop the accent intersection — it's the brand.
Don'tRecolor the accent. Terracotta only.
